FusionX theme edit: show game logos from the RomM plugin
========================================================

Back up each file before editing. Restart Playnite afterwards.

Files (make the same two replacements in both):
    Views\DetailsViewGameOverview.xaml
    Views\GridViewGameOverview.xaml


Replacement 1: where the logo area decides whether to show
------------------------------------------------------------
Find:
    {PluginSettings Plugin=ExtraMetadataLoader, Path=IsLogoAvailable, FallbackValue=False}

Replace with:
    {PluginSettings Plugin=RomM, Path=LogoVisible, FallbackValue=False}


Replacement 2: the logo itself
------------------------------
Find:
    <ContentControl x:Name="ExtraMetadataLoader_LogoLoaderControl" />

Replace with:
    <ContentControl x:Name="RomM_GameLogo" />


Each appears once per file. Leave the other ExtraMetadataLoader lines alone: the ones about
video (VideoLoaderControl, EnableVideoPlayer, IsAnyVideoAvailable) are the trailer player and
simply stay hidden when the extension is not installed.

To undo, swap the two lines back (or restore your backups).
