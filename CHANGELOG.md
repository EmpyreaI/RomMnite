# RomMnite 1.0.0

Release of RomMnite: a Steam-like Playnite for your RomM server, built on the RomM Library Importer.

## Highlights

- Library Home, shelves and a Downloads page; games on several platforms shown once, with a button to switch versions (the one you pick last stays your default while it's installed).
- Emulators downloaded and configured for you, fullscreen, with your controller, rendering at your display's resolution where possible.
- Cloud saves for every supported emulator and, through Ludusavi, Windows games: synced at start-up, around each session and while playing. Offline progress is kept and uploaded on reconnect; conflicting saves are backed up, not lost.
- Achievements from RetroAchievements (through RomM), RPCS3 and Xenia, with pop-ups during play and a sync status on each game.
- In-game overlay on the Home button, for emulated games and borderless or windowed Windows games.
- Several RomM accounts on one PC.
- The RomMnite theme, included.

## Installing

Download the `.pext` below and open it with Playnite. If you used the RomM Library Importer, or a RomMnite test build, uninstall it first; your settings and data are kept.
